# Figure legends

## Figure 1

*File:* `figures/fig1_geographic_distribution.tiff`

**Figure 1** Global distribution of the compiled seed and seedling microbiome dataset.

The map shows sample provenance; the table below lists the contributing studies. Each ring represents one study (BioProject) in one country and is labelled with its project ID and number of samples, so every ring corresponds to a row of the table; the multi-country study PRJEB6729 contributes one ring per country. Ring area is proportional to the number of samples (radius scales with the square root of n), and ring segments give the split between seed and seedling samples. Rings are placed in open space near their country and joined to it by a dotted leader line. Countries contributing samples are shaded teal; countries without samples are grey. The table gives, for each BioProject, the reference, country of origin, host species (abbreviated to genus initial) and the number of seed and seedling samples. Counts are samples retained after quality filtering. Colours follow the Nature Publishing Group palette.

## Figure 2

*File:* `figures/fig_taxa_strips_phylum.tiff`, `figures/fig_taxa_strips_family.tiff`, `figures/fig_taxa_strips_genus.tiff`

**Figure 2** Taxonomic composition of seed and seedling microbiota by study, cultivar and developmental stage.

**a**, Phylum level (9 phyla shown).

**b**, Family level (38 families shown).

**c**, Genus level (51 genera shown).

Each horizontal bar is one cultivar at one developmental stage within a study; studies are grouped in rows labelled by BioProject and reference, ordered by number of samples. Bar segments give the mean of per-sample relative abundances, so every sample carries equal weight. Circles mark seed and triangles seedling samples; n is the number of samples and * marks bars based on fewer than three. Taxa reaching at least 3% mean relative abundance in at least one bar are shown individually; all others are pooled as Other, and reads without an assignment at that rank are shown as Unassigned. Samples with no reads after filtering are excluded. Panels a-c are supplied as separate files.
